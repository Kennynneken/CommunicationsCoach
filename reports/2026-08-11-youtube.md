## What to fix

- **The open (0:00–0:07).** "Oh shoot, I thought it was recording… okay, we're
  gonna restart this, okay" — 7 seconds of throat-clearing on camera. Yes, it's
  cuttable in the edit, but narrating the flub resets your state apologetically;
  the rubric's opening standard is breath → certainty → hook. When a take
  breaks: stop, one deep breath, go again clean. Never explain to the camera.
- **No pause architecture (whole take).** 175 WPM with two sub-0.7s pauses
  means the hook at 0:10 and the tension peak at 0:28 both get steamrolled by
  the next sentence. The Steve Jobs principle from the speech rubric applies
  double on YouTube: the pause after the punchline IS the punchline.
- **Sentence endings (36% final drop).** Lines like "your dream life" (0:18)
  and "into reality" (0:38) end flat/rising. Finish sentences downward — it
  reads as certainty and gives the editor clean cut points.
- **Warmth deficit in the vision passage (0:11–0:20).** You're describing the
  viewer's dream life with a serious face. Let the Duchenne smile you clearly
  have (64% of your smiles are genuine) surface while painting the picture.
