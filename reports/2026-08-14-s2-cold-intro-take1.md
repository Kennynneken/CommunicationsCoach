## THE ONE THING

**Open on what she is doing, not on what she is wearing — and make your second
sentence a question only she could answer.**

Curiosity scored 22 and attunement 30, and they are blocking everything above
them: warmth cannot land through a compliment that reads as appraisal, and
unexpectedness cannot survive the most-used approach in the world. Both unlock
from the same move.

The drill, concretely — **three sentences, in this order:**

1. **Something true about this moment.** What she's doing, holding, reacting to,
   standing next to. Present tense, observed, not prepared.
2. **Your name.** Four words. *"I'm Kenny, by the way."*
3. **One question about her, not about a thing.** It must be unanswerable by a
   noun. "Where'd you get it" is answerable with "Nordstrom." "What made you
   come to this one?" is not.

Then **stop and hold 1.5 seconds.** You are 0 for 9 on deliberate pauses and
0.26s away from your first one.

Rule to test against: **if her best possible answer is one word, the question
was wrong.**
