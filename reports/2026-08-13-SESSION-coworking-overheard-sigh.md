## The headline: tempo finally moved, and it moved everything

`wpm_speaking` across all twelve recorded takes:

`218 · 264 · 176 · 249 · 246 · 214 · 193 · 222 · 242 · 154 · 155 · 108`

Eight takes with **no trend in either direction**. `kenny-patterns.md` had
called it "the top priority, and it has never been genuinely drilled." It was
named as THE ONE THING after take 1 and dropped 88 WPM on the very next rep,
then held through a longer take, then fell another 46 to a career best of
**107.7** — below the 110–150 power zone, which on a six-word question is
correct. Total movement: **134 WPM in three takes.** Previous twelve-take floor
was 176.

**And melody is a pure function of it.** Four takes, one variable:

| `wpm_speaking` | 242 | 154 | 155 | 108 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **pitch variance** | **77** | **100** | 84 | **100** |
| **pitch range (Hz)** | 72–163 | 86–352 | 87–182 | 73–235 |

The 77 in take 1 is the lowest variance score he has ever posted; his native
baseline is 90–100. He never once thought about melody — he slowed down and his
best instrument switched itself back on. This is the most useful causal finding
of the session: **pitch variance is not a skill to train, it is a symptom of
tempo.** Fix the speed and the melody is free.

## The new finding: the relocation reflex

The most valuable diagnosis of the session, and it only became visible because
the scene ran four beats instead of one:

| Take | Her move | His answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sighs, available | *"Seems like you need some **space**"* |
| 2 | "It's my own fault." | *"Why don't we **go get some warm coffee**"* |
| 3 | "It has a name and a date." | *"Let's **get up, move our bodies around**"* |

Three for three: every time she moved toward the real thing, he proposed that
somebody physically move. Take 2 scored 80 on unexpectedness for exactly this —
in isolation it reads as a bold frame change. On the third repetition it is
avoidance wearing the costume of leadership. **A single-take session would have
scored it as a strength and never caught it.** He broke it in take 4 under the
strongest pull yet.
