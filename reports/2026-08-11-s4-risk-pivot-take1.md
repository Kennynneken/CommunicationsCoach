## What to fix

- **00:06 — "That's really cool."** Empty praise, and it costs him a beat. It's
  the verbal equivalent of nodding. Cut it and the question lands twice as hard.
- **00:07 — "How?"** A false start he abandoned and immediately talked over.
  That's where the pause belonged.
- **He asked origin, not cost.** She handed him a live wire — *"telling people
  why they can't do the thing they want to do"* — and the sharper question was
  what that does to her *now*: "Does being the 'no' person all day wear on you?"

- **240 WPM under the hood.** The tempo win from take 3 did not survive contact
  with a question he cared about.
- **Smile 23.5%, flat exactly on the question.** Warm words, cold face.
- **Four takes. Still no name.** She has now told him her job, her department,

## THE ONE THING

**Ask the follow-up, then shut up and let the silence do the work — she is about
to give you something real and your job is to not step on it.** Current: 0 pauses
inside speech across four takes, 240 WPM local, and a habit of filling
(*"That's really cool," "How?"*) instead of waiting. Target next take: after her
answer, **one follow-up question drawn from a specific word she just said, then
a full 1.5 seconds of silence before anything else comes out of your mouth.**
Count it. The question that goes three layers deep is the one you ask *after*
you've let her finish thinking.
