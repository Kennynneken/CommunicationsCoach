## THE ONE THING

**Put the smile ON the words, not around them. Specifically: be smiling before
the punchline starts, and hold it through the end of the sentence.**

Last session's ONE THING was the deliberate pause. It has not moved in ten
takes and it stays open — but it is not what is capping *this* beat. Warmth has
the lowest ceiling in his entire profile (never once above 74 in eleven takes),
and the reason is now measured rather than guessed: his warmest expression
reliably arrives **after** he finishes speaking. He is not failing to be warm.
He is failing to be warm *on time*.

The drill, and it is mechanical:

1. **Smile first. Then open your mouth.** Not simultaneously — the smile leads
   by about half a second. This is the same structure as your pre-turn hold,
   which you are already good at.
2. **The last word of the sentence gets more smile than the first, not less.**
   Your curve runs 0.61 → 0.00. Invert it.
3. **Test it on this exact line.** Say "rainbow shoes to an event like this"
   twice — once with your face at rest, once smiling through the word *shoes*.
   Those are two different sentences and only one of them is an opener.

You have already proven you can produce a 0.82 Duchenne smile in this take. You
produced it 0.4 seconds too late. Move it left.
