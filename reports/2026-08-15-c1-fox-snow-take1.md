## What to fix

- **He commented on her, not on the moment** — the one law on the C1 card. A
  compliment on appearance is an evaluation, and an evaluation puts her in the
  position of receiving a verdict from a stranger. "Comment on the shared
  moment" makes her a co-conspirator instead. Same breath, completely different
  social position.
- **The sentence is a dead end by construction.** "Hey, your jacket's really
  cool" → "Oh, thanks." Loop closed. There is nothing in it for her to pick up,
  no question, no opinion to disagree with, no hook. Contrast his own best
  material: "I don't try to be good. I try to be real." — that one *demands* a
  response.
- **He ignored everything actually in the scene** — 00:00, attunement 25. The
  library book, the pastry case she'd read twice, and the not-on-her-phone
  signal were all handed to him. He reached past all three for a garment that

- **Upward final** — `sentence_final_drop_pct` 0%. Land the last word down.
- **Flat face on "really cool"** — 2.0s and 2.67s at 0.00 smile.

## THE ONE THING

**Open on something that is actually in front of you, and land the last word
downward.**

Not a compliment. Not about her. One concrete detail from the shared moment —
the thing she's holding, the thing you're both waiting on, the absurd thing
happening ten feet away — said as a *statement of your own opinion*, with the
final word falling.

Current: attunement 25, novelty 18, `sentence_final_drop_pct` 0%.
Target next take: **one detail from the actual scene, and final drop ≥ 50%.**

The drill: before you speak, name three things you can actually see. Open on
one of them. Then drop your voice on the last word like you're setting something
down on a table — not handing it to her for approval.

This is aimed at attunement (25) rather than curiosity (15) because attunement
is the blocker: the moment he opens on something real in the room, novelty,
curiosity and depth all become reachable in the same sentence. Opening on her
jacket makes all three impossible no matter how well he delivers it.
