# Coaching Report — 2026-08-11 — test-talking-head-40s.mp4 **[SAMPLE — pipeline dry run]**

> This is the Phase 5 dry-run report, written from the analysis packet of a
> 40-second public-domain 1984 Oval Office address clip to prove the loop
> works. Not a Kenny session — kept as a worked example of the report format.

**Context:** speech
**Score: 62/100** (no prior speech session — baseline)
*Professional, composed, zero filler — but rushed at 186 WPM with almost no
deliberate pausing, so the words inform without ever landing emotionally.*

## What the numbers show

- **WPM 186** — well above the 110–150 power zone for speeches. The rubric
  reads >170 as nervous; here it reads as a radio-address cadence that never
  lets a line breathe.
- **Pauses: 5 total, only 1 deliberate (≥0.8s at a sentence end), p90 0.78s**
  against a rubric target of ≥1.2s at story beats. Zero filler-bridged pauses
  (excellent — silence, never "um"), but the silences are too short to command.
- **Filler: 0/min.** Genuinely elite; composure is not the problem.
- **Pitch variance score 45** with sentence_final_drop 100% — every sentence
  ends with a confident downward cadence, and intensity trail-off is 0%.
  Endings land; the melody between them is only moderate.
- **Body:** uprightness 0.67, trend improving (+0.14), sway 27 (low), smile 0%,
  gaze-to-camera 63% / down 37% — he is reading from a script, which caps
  connection in an on-camera setting.
- **Incongruence flags: none.** Voice and body agree; nothing rings false.

## What landed

- 00:29 — the one truly deliberate pause (1.0s, silent) right after "…San
  Francisco Bay." — the geographic crescendo gets a beat to settle. This is
  the pattern the whole speech needed.
- Sentence endings 100% downward, 0% trail-off — every line finishes with
  authority instead of fading (e.g. 00:14 "…and stifle that growth.").
- Zero filler across 40 seconds of dense argument — composed under load.

## What to fix

- 186 WPM sustained the entire clip (wpm_by_minute flat) — no tempo contrast
  between argument and punchline.
- Only 1 of 5 sentence boundaries got a commanding pause; 00:04 and 00:17 both
  deserved ≥1.2s and got 0.68s.
- 37% of gaze is down at the script; smile 0% — fine for a stern address, but
  a warmth beat at 00:30 ("make our free country freer still") would have
  earned one.

## THE ONE THING

**At every sentence that completes an idea, hold a full 1.2-second silent
pause before starting the next sentence.** Current: p90 pause 0.78s, one
deliberate pause in 40 seconds. Target: p90 ≥ 1.2s, four deliberate pauses
per minute. This alone would also pull the 186 WPM down toward the 150 power
zone without consciously slowing any words.
