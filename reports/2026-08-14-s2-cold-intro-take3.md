## THE ONE THING

**Ask one question, then close your mouth and do not open it until someone
else has spoken.**

Presence is the lowest dimension (30) and this take proves it is the one
blocking the others. Curiosity did not fall 34 points because he ran out of
questions — he had one out at 2.37 seconds. It fell because he filled the
answer space himself 0.64 seconds later. Attunement can't score either: you
cannot read a room you never let respond.

Eleven takes, eleven zeros on deliberate pauses, and three consecutive takes
today with **literally zero silence anywhere inside them** — 3.62s of talking
in a 3.62s span, then 5.7 in 5.7.

The drill for take 4, in order:

1. **Smile — half a beat early**, as you did at 0.33s. Keep it.
2. **Name first, and land it downward.** *"I'm Kenny."* Full stop, pitch drops.
   Four words, and it buys you the right to ask for something.
3. **One question about them.** Not about you, not about their shoes.
4. **Then stop. Count one-one-thousand-two.** The clip should end with you
   silent and still, looking at them.

The take is *supposed* to end in silence. If your recording ends the instant
your last word does, you have not done the rep.
