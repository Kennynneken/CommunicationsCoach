## What genuinely changed

- **The warmth ceiling broke.** Twelve takes ≤74 all-time, then 75 in take 4 —
  on smile *timing*, the exact mechanism the research named. His biggest
  smile moved from 0.4s after speech to dead-center on "I'm Kenny."
- **The pause exists now.** Fourteen takes of `deliberate_count = 0` across
  two sessions, then four consecutive takes with real silence architecture:
  0.82s → 1.04s → 1.32s → 1.3s deliberate pauses, lead-ins to 2.93s, and a
  passing take that was ~58% silence. Installed under drill conditions;
  unproven unprompted — that's the test next session.
- **Presence: last session's average was 45. Take 11 posted 84.**
- **Melody proved steerable in both directions.** Elite (86–100) on light
  beats → collapsed to an all-time-low 42 as content got heavy (he equated
  serious with flat) → recovered to 100 *within the grave register* on one
  instruction. "Quiet, not flat" is now a proven command.
- **The burial mechanism was suppressed.** Three early takes lost their best
  question to stacking or self-answer; the last four takes all ended on a
  single unstacked question.
- **Mastery mode works.** 61 → 74 → 77 → 81 on one instruction per attempt,
  with zero regression between attempts — every prior instruction held while
  the new one was added. This is the strongest evidence yet for his
  one-element learning profile.
