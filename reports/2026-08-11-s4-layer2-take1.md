## THE ONE THING

**Housekeeping goes before depth, never after — once you ask a real question,
the next sound in the conversation is hers.** Current: best question of the
session at 00:05, stepped on at 00:08 by an eight-second name introduction, and
0 deliberate pauses in five takes. Target next take: **ask, then count 1.5
seconds of silence in your head before your mouth is allowed to open.** If she
starts talking during the count, you win. Nothing — no name, no "I see," no
"that's really cool" — goes into that gap.
