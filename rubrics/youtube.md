# Rubric — YouTube / On-Camera Video

100 = historic-best on-camera communicator; the lens feels like a person.

## Weighting
- Camera connection (gaze toward_camera_pct — target 70%+; stable, not darting) — 25%
- Energy & melody (on camera, flat reads flatter: variance_score target 70+;
  "energy generosity" — the lens eats ~20% of your energy, send more than
  feels natural) — 25%
- Openings (first 15 seconds: breath, certainty, hook — no throat-clearing) — 20%
- Face & posture (smile ON the words, Duchenne if available, upright, no
  collapse over take; torso in frame, gestures visible in the Power Sphere) — 20%
- Editing-resistant delivery (clean sentence endings, low filler, deliberate
  pauses that survive cuts) — 10%

## Notes
- This is the ONE context where eye contact is directly measurable: report
  toward_camera_pct against the 70% target explicitly.
- Score the first 15 seconds separately in every report — retention lives there.
- trail_off_sentences_pct matters double on camera; mics catch every fade.
- posture start_vs_end_delta: takes often collapse; flag any delta < -0.10.
- Smile timing is ruthless on camera: the audience sees the warm expression
  arrive after the warm sentence as clearly as a bad edit. Face in the emotion
  DURING the words (tonality follows the face).
- Rate contrast survives cuts; a single unvarying rate makes every cut feel
  the same. Mark the moments where tempo changed on purpose.
