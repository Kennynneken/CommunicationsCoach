# Rubric — Speech / Presentation

100 = Caesar, Obama, Teddy Roosevelt. A delivery historians would study.

## Weighting
- Pause mastery & rhythm — 25%
- Vocal command (melody, projection, sentence endings that land) — 20%
- Emotional arc (the audience FEELS a journey, not receives information) — 20%
- Body language (upright, still, commanding; gestures on emphasis) — 20%
- Composure & certainty (breath before starting, no anxious gaps, low filler) — 15%

## What the metrics mean here
- deliberate_count high + anxious_gap_count low = command. Target p90 pause >= 1.2s
  at story beats, delivered silent (bridged_by_filler = false).
- WPM: 110–150 is the power zone for speeches; >170 reads as nervous.
- Intensity should NOT trail off at sentence ends; trail_off_sentences_pct < 15%.
- Long pauses are an ASSET in this context. Score them up, not down.
- Low sway + gesture_active spiking on emphasized words = world-class pattern.

## Common score bands
- 80s: professional keynote quality; pauses deliberate, zero anxious gaps
- 60s: competent but informational; rhythm exists, emotion flat
- 40s: readable delivery, rushed, filler-bridged pauses, energy trails off
