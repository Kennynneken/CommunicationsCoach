# Rubric — Speech / Presentation

100 = Caesar, Obama, Teddy Roosevelt. A delivery historians would study.

## Weighting
- Pause mastery & rhythm — 25%
- Vocal command (melody, rate/volume contrast, sentence endings that land) — 20%
- Emotional arc (the audience FEELS a journey, not receives information) — 20%
- Body language (upright, commanding; gestures in the Power Sphere on
  emphasis) — 20%
- Composure & certainty (breath before starting, no anxious gaps, low filler) — 15%

## What the metrics mean here
- deliberate_count high + anxious_gap_count low = command. Target p90 pause >= 1.2s
  at story beats, delivered silent (bridged_by_filler = false). Long pauses are
  an ASSET in this context — they amplify the emotion of the line that
  preceded them (Giang). Score them up, not down.
- **WPM: 110–150 is the power zone for prepared remarks** (this zone applies to
  speeches/toasts ONLY — conversational contexts score contrast, not a zone).
  >170 sustained reads as nervous. But WITHIN the zone, flat is still flat:
  score rate contrast — a deliberate slow-down or held silence on the beat
  that matters is the actual skill the zone is a proxy for.
- Volume contrast is a tool: dropping volume on the emotional line pulls the
  room in (Giang); one loudness throughout is monotone's cousin.
- Intensity should NOT trail off at sentence ends; trail_off_sentences_pct < 15%
  (trail-off = running out of air; keep energy first word to last).
- Storytelling: **relive, don't report** (present tense, body joins in,
  senses + one emotion); land the meaning explicitly ("the reason I'm telling
  you this…"). Stories capped at 2–3 minutes.
- Low sway + gesture_active spiking on emphasized words = world-class pattern.
  Gestures live belly-button-to-eyes and depict content; zero gesture through
  a story means the body was left out of it.

## Common score bands
- 80s: professional keynote quality; pauses deliberate, zero anxious gaps,
  audible rate/volume contrast at the beats
- 60s: competent but informational; rhythm exists, emotion flat
- 40s: readable delivery, rushed, filler-bridged pauses, energy trails off
