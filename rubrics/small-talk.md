# Rubric — Small Talk / Conversation

100 = Casanova-level, historic-best conversational influence.

## Weighting
- Emotional depth achieved (moved past informational; 3 layers deep) — 30%
- Vocal melody & warmth (pitch variance, smile audible in the voice) — 25%
- Turn dynamics (breath/pause before speaking, comfortable with silence) — 15%
- Novelty (out-of-the-ordinary questions; not autopilot) — 15%
- Body language (open, smiling, eye contact if camera setup allows) — 15%

## What the metrics + transcript mean here
- Run the 3-layer test on the transcript: question -> answer -> deeper follow-up
  -> deeper again. Count layers reached per thread; cite the exact moment he bailed.
- Classify each of his questions: informational / emotional / identity. Flag
  autopilot questions ("where are you from", "what do you do") when not followed
  by an emotional pivot.
- pitch variance_score should be HIGH here — target 65+. Monotone is deadly in
  small talk even when acceptable in a briefing.
- Pauses read differently here: mid-conversation silences > 2s are usually a
  liability. Short relaxed pauses before HIS turns are an asset.
- Talk-time ratio (when diarization detects 2 speakers): 40–60% is healthy;
  flag > 70% (steamrolling) or < 25% (passenger).

## Common score bands
- 80s: partner audibly lights up; multiple 3-layer threads; melodic delivery
- 60s: pleasant, one emotional pivot, but threads die at layer 1–2
- 40s: interview mode — question, answer, next question; flat melody
