# Rubric — Small Talk / Conversation

100 = historic-best conversational connection: the person leaves feeling
chosen, understood, and better about themselves — and retells the conversation
to someone else that week.

## Weighting
- Depth achieved (past informational; 3 layers; deep questions) — 25%
- Attunement (conversation-type matching, mood/energy matching, looping) — 25%
- Vocal warmth & melody (pitch variance, smile audible in the voice, smile ON
  the words) — 20%
- Reciprocity (the braid: follow-ups ↔ self-disclosure; neither interrogation
  nor monologue) — 15%
- Composure & turn dynamics (breath before speaking, comfortable with silence
  after his questions) — 15%

## What the metrics + transcript mean here
- **Run the matching test first (Duhigg):** classify each of HER bids —
  practical / emotional / identity — and check whether his response joined the
  same conversation. An emotional bid answered practically is the canonical
  miss even when the words are kind. "Helped, hugged, or heard?"
- Run the 3-layer test on the transcript: question → answer → deeper follow-up
  → deeper again. Count layers per thread; cite the exact moment he bailed.
  Deep questions ask about feelings about life, not facts of life: values,
  decisions, experiences ("What made you…", "What was it like…", "Are you glad
  you…").
- **Count follow-up questions separately** (built from her exact words). They
  are the single most likability-predictive move (Huang et al.).
- **Check the braid (Aron/Smithyman):** disclosure must alternate. All
  questions = liked-not-known cap (~70). All statements = monologue cap. His
  disclosures should carry motive/contrast/current-motion/example — one layer
  below surface, personal not private.
- **Looping:** did he ever repeat her meaning back in his own words and check
  it? ("So it sounds like X — right?") Score it up strongly when present at a
  real moment; it is the rarest observable skill.
- pitch variance_score should be HIGH here — target 65+. Monotone is deadly in
  small talk. Melody = memorability (Giang: 2 notes vs 88 keys).
- **Smile timing over smile quantity**: warmth must land DURING the warm words.
  A Duchenne smile arriving after the sentence is warmth delivered late.
- **Laughter is a probe** (Provine/NASA): when she laughs, matched intensity =
  connection accepted; polite chuckle or flat face = declined. Check what his
  face and energy do in the 2 seconds after her laughter.
- Pauses read differently here than in speeches: mid-conversation silences
  > 2s are usually a liability — but **the silence after HIS question is an
  asset, not a gap.** Never let him answer his own question; cite it when he
  does. Short relaxed pauses before his turns are composure (Strategic Pause).
- Tempo: score contrast, not a zone. One unvarying rate scores down at any
  speed; a deliberate slow-down on the sentence that matters scores up.
- Talk-time ratio (when diarization detects 2 speakers): 40–60% is healthy;
  flag > 70% (steamrolling) or < 25% (passenger/interrogator).

## Common score bands
- 80s: partner audibly lights up; ≥1 three-layer thread; braided disclosure;
  at least one loop or perfect type-match at an emotional moment
- 60s: pleasant, one emotional pivot, but threads die at layer 1–2, or the
  braid is one-sided (all asks / all tells)
- 40s: interview mode — question, answer, next question; flat melody; type
  mismatches (practical answers to emotional bids)
